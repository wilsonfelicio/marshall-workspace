Day by Day
Wed Sep 30 Arrival
▶
Land at CPT. Pick up rental car. Fit the car seats before you drive (rear-facing for Alice — it's the law under 9kg)
~20 min easy drive to the V&A Waterfront Airbnb. Check in, unpack
Groceries at Checkers / Woolworths in the Waterfront — diapers, baby basics, water
Decompress. 5-hour time jump (CPT is UTC+2) — go easy.
Easy evening: stroll the harbour, watch the boats. Dinner at the V&A Food Market. Early night
Thu Oct 1 Waterfront
▶
9:30 Two Oceans Aquarium — right by the Airbnb. Penguins, kelp forest, touch pools, downstairs kids' play area. Under-4s free
11:30 Al-fresco lunch on the Waterfront — harbour deck or the Food Market
13:00-15:00 — Nap. No driving today, let everyone land.
15:30 Green Point Urban Park (5 min) — big playground, lawns, biodiversity garden. Francisco runs, Alice on a blanket
17:00 Sunset walk along the Sea Point Promenade
Fri Oct 2 Table Mountain
▶
⚑ Floating anchor — Table Mountain is wind-dependent. Check the cableway webcam/status that morning; if it's windy, swap this with any calm, clear day. Don't save it for the last day.
8:30 Drive to the Lower Cableway (15 min). Pre-booked tickets, go early — calmest wind, clearest views, smallest crowds, before nap
9:00 Cable car up. Flat paved paths on top — use the carrier, not the stroller, and keep the kids well clear of the edges
11:30 Down. Drive to Camps Bay — beachfront lunch facing the Twelve Apostles (sea is cold, but the strip is lovely before the afternoon wind)
13:30-15:00 — Nap back at the Airbnb
16:00 Easy evening at the Waterfront
Sat Oct 3 Penguins
▶
9:00 Drive to Boulders Beach, Simon's Town (~40 min, False Bay side)
9:45 Foxy Beach boardwalk — stroller-accessible, penguins a metre away. Francisco will lose his mind
12:00 Lunch in Kalk Bay — Harbour House / Live Bait literally over the water. Browse the village shops
13:30 Quick stop at Muizenberg — the famous colourful beach huts, gentle warm-water beach for a paddle
Kids nap in the car on the drive back
16:30 Home, easy evening
Sun Oct 4 Kirstenbosch
▶
9:30 Drive to Kirstenbosch Botanical Garden (~20 min)
10:00 The Boomslang canopy walkway (stroller-friendly), then turn the kids loose on the huge safe lawns. Bring a picnic blanket
12:00 Picnic on the lawns, or the garden tea-room/restaurant
13:30-15:00 — Nap
15:30 Blue Train Park (Mouille Point) — mini train + jungle gym, perfect for Francisco. Or just the Sea Point promenade
Mon Oct 5 Cape Point
▶
⚑ The most ambitious day — pick a calm one. Baboons at Cape Point are aggressive: windows up, no visible food, don't carry snacks openly.
8:30 Drive the Atlantic side via Chapman's Peak Drive (toll, check it's open — closes for wind/rockfall). One of the world's great coastal drives
10:30 Cape of Good Hope / Cape Point — the Flying Dutchman funicular up to the lighthouse saves the steep climb. Photo at the sign
12:30 Picnic, then on the way back stop at Imhoff Farm (Kommetjie) — goats, alpacas, camel rides, relaxed. Great toddler decompress
Kids nap in the car
16:30 Home. Big day done — order in
Tue Oct 6 Slow day
▶
10:00 Deliberate downtime after Cape Point. Hout Bay (~25 min) — short seal-island boat trip (~40 min, calm-water, kid-friendly) to see thousands of Cape fur seals
12:00 Casual lunch at the Hout Bay harbour
13:30-15:00 — Nap
15:30 V&A Waterfront at your own pace — Cape Wheel, Scratch Patch, harbour seals, ice cream. Zero driving
Wed Oct 7 Winelands
▶
10:00 Drive to Spier (Stellenbosch) (~40 min) — the most kid-friendly wine farm: rolling lawns, the Elemental play garden, farm animals
11:30 Long al-fresco lunch — wine for the grown-ups, room for Francisco to roam, Alice on a blanket. No rush
⚑ Closer alternative if you want less car time: Groot Constantia (25 min) — book a picnic basket on the lawn 24h ahead.
Kids nap in the car on the way home
16:00 Home, easy evening
Thu Oct 8 Whales
▶
⚑ Peak southern-right whale season — your dates are prime. Land-based cliff path beats a boat with little ones (no seasickness, leave anytime).
9:00 Drive to Hermanus (~1h45, scenic Whale Coast). The world's best land-based whale watching
11:00 Walk a stretch of the Cliff Path — southern rights breach close to shore, sometimes within metres. Go at your own pace
12:30 Lunch in Hermanus, then drive back
⚑ If the round trip feels like too much: you'll likely already have spotted whales in False Bay near Simon's Town/Cape Point — swap this for a beach/rest day.
16:30 Home
Fri Oct 9 Flex + favourites
▶
Morning Buffer day. Catch Table Mountain if wind stole it earlier, or redo a favourite — penguins, aquarium, Muizenberg beach
Option Grown-up bit: Bo-Kaap colourful houses + Company's Garden (squirrels, easy with the kids)
13:00-15:00 — Nap
15:30 Last V&A / Sea Point sunset. Pack once the kids are down
Sat Oct 10 Departure
▶
Morning Final breakfast at the Waterfront
Return the rental car at CPT — leave buffer for refuel + pulling the car seats out
Fly home. ✈️

Packing Essentials
  • Passports (check 6-month validity + blank pages)
  • Kids' birth certificates (SA may ask for minors)
  • Health insurance cards (all 4)
  • Confirm rental car seats — or bring your own (rear-facing for Alice)
  • Layers + fleece (cool 9-14°C mornings)
  • Windbreaker + light rain shell
  • Strong sun protection — hats + sunscreen (UV high even when cool)
  • Closed shoes (Cape Point / Kirstenbosch paths)
  • Swim gear + towels (sea cold, pools warm)
  • Stroller + baby carrier (carrier for Table Mountain)
  • Diapers Alice (~8/day = 90) + supplies
  • Baby meds (antitérmico, soro, termômetro)
  • Universal adapter (SA Type M/N plugs)
