📰 Storylines — Opening Days

  • Biggest World Cup ever kicks off. 48 teams, 104 matches, 16 cities across three countries, ending July 19. The new round-of-32 means the eight best third-placed teams survive the group stage — finishing third is no longer elimination.
  • Mexico open with a statement. Hosts beat South Africa 2–0 at a roaring Azteca, Julián Quiñones with the tournament's first goal inside nine minutes. Group A looks Mexico's to lose.
  • Brazil's path starts Saturday vs Morocco — a banana-skin opener. Morocco were 2022 semifinalists and remain organised and dangerous on the break. Haiti and Scotland complete Group C; Brazil are clear favourites to top it but the Morocco game sets the tone.
  • Argentina, France, Spain, England headline the favourites. Holders Argentina are in Group J with Austria, Algeria and Jordan — a kind draw. England drew Croatia, Ghana and Panama in Group L.
  • Group of death watch. Group H — Spain, Uruguay, Cape Verde, Saudi Arabia — and Group K — Portugal, Colombia, DR Congo, Uzbekistan — are the early picks for tightest sections.
  • US, Canada, Mexico all chasing knockout football at home. Co-hosts get crowd and travel advantages; how far they go shapes the tournament's energy.
