📰 Storylines — Opening Days

  • Brazil's opener is tomorrow — and it's a test. Brazil vs Morocco, Sat 19:00 BRT at MetLife. Morocco were 2022 semifinalists: organised, lethal on the break, the toughest first opponent any contender drew. Our sim still makes Brazil 9.1% to win it all (#3 behind Spain and France), with a 99% chance to clear the group — but the Morocco game sets the tone.
  • Mexico open with a statement. Hosts beat South Africa 2–0 at a roaring Azteca — Julián Quiñones with the tournament's first goal inside nine minutes, Raúl Jiménez adding the second. Group A looks Mexico's to lose.
  • South Korea come from behind. Czechia led through Ladislav Krejčí (59'), but Hwang In-beom levelled (67') and Oh Hyeon-gyu won it (80') in Guadalajara. Two wins from two in Group A on opening day; Czechia, pre-tournament the group's #2 seed, already chasing.
  • Co-hosts up today. Canada host Bosnia in Toronto (16:00 BRT) — market makes them ~52% favourites but Bosnia sit deep, so expect tight. USA open vs Paraguay at SoFi (22:00 BRT), ~54% with the crowd behind them against a disciplined Paraguay defence. Both nations chasing knockout football at home.
  • Favourites in waiting. Spain (19%) and France (15%) head our title board and don't start until next week — Spain vs Cape Verde on the 15th, France later. Holders Argentina sit 6th at ~5% in a kind Group J draw.
  • Round-of-32 cushion. The expanded format means the eight best third-placed teams survive the group stage — finishing third is no longer automatic elimination, which changes how the big sides can manage their third group game.
