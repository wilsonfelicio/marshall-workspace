💡 Interpretation

Gold (β=1.57): Dominant driver by far. 1 standard deviation move in gold → 3.6σ move in GDX. Miners are levered gold.

EEM (β=0.50): Significant EM equity exposure. Miners are global-cyclical, not just gold.

DXY (β=-0.62): Strong negative dollar beta. Weak dollar = strong miners. This is partially collinear with gold but adds explanatory power independently.

FXI (β=-0.18): Negative China exposure after controlling for EM. Miners benefit from China weakness — possibly via capital rotation from China into real assets.

TLT, TIP, DBC: Near zero. Rate duration and broad commodities add nothing once you have gold + equity + FX factors. Don't hedge GDX with bonds.
