Street circuit down the Las Vegas Strip, raced counterclockwise at night past the Bellagio, Caesars Palace, the Venetian and around the Sphere. Average speeds in Monza territory — the ~1.9 km Strip blast is one of the longest flat-out runs in F1, ending in the hardest braking zone of the season at T14. Low-downforce setups, long slipstreams, cold asphalt: the field compresses and overtaking is genuinely easy by modern standards. Lap record 1:33.365 (Verstappen, 2025). The 2026 cars' active aero (no DRS flaps — X-mode straights) makes the tow game even bigger this year.

Vegas, take two. F1 first came here in 1981–82 as the Caesars Palace Grand Prix — a widely mocked circuit laid out in a casino car park, which still managed to decide the 1981 title (Piquet, by one point, nearly passing out from heat exhaustion). The modern Strip Circuit arrived in 2023 as Liberty Media's $500m+ flagship project: a Saturday-night race under the casino lights, with the paddock inside a purpose-built pit building F1 actually owns.
Winners' roll: Verstappen ('23), Russell ('24), Verstappen ('25). Russell's 2024 win was a Mercedes 1-2 with Hamilton carving from P10 — the race where the car finally worked in the cold. Verstappen clinched his fourth title here in 2024 and won again in 2025, the night both McLarens were thrown out post-race for excessive skid-block wear — a disqualification that reshaped that title run-in.
Where to pass: T14 at the end of the Strip is the banker — ~350 km/h down to second gear, with a tow the length of three football pitches before it. T1 off the start/finish straight is move two, and the run to T5 before the Sphere section gives a third look. Cold track + long straights = brutal front-graining and big tyre-delta passes late in stints. Expect 40+ overtakes; the 2023 race had more lead changes than any race that season.
Iconic Vegas Moments
  • 🕳️ 2023 — The manhole cover: Nine minutes into the first-ever FP1, Sainz's Ferrari hit a loose water-valve cover on the Strip at ~320 km/h, destroying the chassis, battery and engine. FP2 ran at 2:30 AM in front of empty grandstands after fans were sent home. The weekend recovered into one of the best races of the year — Verstappen winning after a three-way fight with Leclerc, who passed Pérez on the final lap for P2.
  • 🎰 1981 — Title decided in a car park: The original Vegas race: Nelson Piquet, suffering heat exhaustion so severe he needed help out of the car, finished P5 to beat Carlos Reutemann to the championship by a single point. Reutemann, starting from pole, faded to P8 and never fully explained why.
  • 🥶 2024 — Russell's cold-night masterclass: Pole and a controlled win in 11°C track temps that crippled half the field's tyre warm-up, leading a Mercedes 1-2. Three corners down the road, Verstappen cruised to P5 — enough for world title number four, celebrated in a sombrero.
  • ⚖️ 2025 — McLaren's double DSQ: Verstappen won on the road, then the stewards found both McLarens' skid blocks worn beyond the legal limit. Norris and Piastri were excluded, handing Max maximum damage in the title fight. One of the most consequential technical disqualifications in modern F1.
  • 🚀 2026 — First Vegas for the new cars: The 2026 regulations' active aero means no conventional DRS — straight-line "X-mode" is available to everyone, everywhere. On a track that's half full-throttle, nobody quite knows how big the slipstream effect will be. Teams expect the lowest drag levels of the season.

📰 Key Stories Heading Into Las Vegas

  • Season finale saved — Vegas opens the last triple-header — After months of doubt over the Middle East situation, F1 confirmed this week that Qatar (Nov 27–29) and Abu Dhabi (Dec 4–6) go ahead as planned, and Imola — on standby as a replacement finale — has been told to stand down. Had they fallen through, Vegas would have been the season finale. Instead it's round 22 of 24, with 75 points still on the table.
  • Both titles are gone — the P2 fight is the championship now — Antonelli wrapped the drivers title in Mexico; Mercedes sealed the constructors in the Brazil rain (652 pts, first since 2021, a full title double). What's left is genuinely tight: Verstappen 279, Russell 262, Hamilton 261, Leclerc 249, Norris 246 — five drivers covered by 33 points for P2, three races to go. Max is the form pick and owns this track: two wins and the lap record.
  • Norris wins the rain lottery at Interlagos — Lando survived a late downpour to win in Brazil ahead of Verstappen — who drove from 21st on the grid to P2 — with Hadjar taking his first Red Bull podium in P3. It was McLaren's reminder that the car is still quick; Vegas was also the scene of their 2025 double disqualification, so there's a score to settle on the Strip.
  • Red Bull vs McLaren for P3 — 17 points — Red Bull 413, McLaren 396. Verstappen and Hadjar are out-scoring the papaya cars at nearly every round this autumn, and prize-money positions are worth tens of millions. Vegas's cold tarmac historically suits neither perfectly — but Max has never finished off the podium here.
  • Cadillac's biggest weekend yet — The American team's home-market showcase, still chasing its first point with Pérez and Bottas. Vegas's attrition rate (safety cars in all three editions) is their best shot left this season.
