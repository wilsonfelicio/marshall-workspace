America's first purpose-built F1 track (2012), designed as a greatest-hits album: the T2–T9 esses copy Silverstone's Maggotts–Becketts, the stadium section channels Hockenheim, and the triple-apex T16–18 is Istanbul's Turn 8. The signature is Turn 1 — a 133-ft climb into a blind left hairpin, the best lap-one corner of the season. Real passing happens into T1 and at the end of the 1.2 km back straight into T12. Counterclockwise, famously bumpy (built on shifting Texas clay), and hard on necks and tyres alike. Lap record 1:36.169 (Leclerc, 2019).

The American revival. After decades of failed US venues (the Caesars Palace parking lot, the 2005 Indianapolis six-car farce), COTA finally gave F1 a real American home in 2012 — a Hermann Tilke design that actually works, built into the hills southeast of Austin. It's hosted every US GP since, drawing 400k+ weekend crowds, and is now the opening act of an American triple bill with Mexico City and São Paulo. The track rewards all-rounder cars: high-speed change of direction in sector 1, a drag-race sector 2, and a technical stadium final sector.
Winners' roll: Hamilton ×5 ('12, '14, '15, '16, '17), Verstappen ×4 ('21, '22, '23, '25), Räikkönen ('18), Bottas ('19), Leclerc ('24). Of the current grid, Hamilton, Verstappen and Leclerc have won here. Verstappen won last year ahead of Norris and Leclerc, and a fifth win this weekend would tie Hamilton's record at the track.
Where to pass: Two genuine zones: the uphill braking duel into T1 (300+ km/h to a blind 2nd-gear hairpin — divebomb central on lap 1) and the end of the 1.2 km back straight into T12, the main DRS overtaking spot. T11's hairpin exit decides the T12 run, so battles chain through both. The esses punish anyone who slides wide, and track-limits policing at T9 and T19 generates penalties almost every year.
Iconic COTA Moments
  • 🏁 2012 — The inaugural duel: Hamilton hunted down Vettel and passed him with DRS on the back straight — the last win of his McLaren career, at the first-ever COTA race. 117,000 Texans showed up on a Sunday and the US GP was suddenly alive again.
  • 🧢 2015 — Title in the rain, cap in the face: In a chaotic wet-dry race, Hamilton muscled Rosberg off at Turn 1, pounced on his late mistake, and clinched his third championship. Rosberg flung the podium cap back at him in the cooldown room — the moment their cold war went public.
  • ⏱️ 2017 — The podium-room penalty: Verstappen passed Räikkönen for P3 on the final lap, cutting T17 — and was stripped of the podium while already in the cooldown room. "Mickey Mouse penalty," said Max. Track limits at COTA have been radioactive ever since.
  • 🦊 2018 — The Iceman's last stand: Räikkönen, 39, won from P2 — his first victory in 113 races and five years, and the final one of his career. It also postponed Hamilton's title party. The entire paddock applauded.
  • ⚔️ 2021 — Max vs Lewis, round 17: The peak of their title war: Verstappen won off an aggressive two-stop undercut, holding off Hamilton's fresher tyres by 1.3s after 20 laps of pursuit. Widely rated the strategic race of that season.
  • 💥 2024 — Ferrari 1-2 and the T12 flashpoint: Leclerc led Sainz home in a Ferrari sweep, while Verstappen and Norris banged wheels at T12 late on — Norris passed off-track, got 5 seconds, and the track-limits argument consumed F1 for a fortnight.

📰 Key Stories Heading Into Austin

  • Match point: Antonelli can be champion on Sunday — The equation: he wins, Russell finishes 9th or lower, Hamilton 5th or lower. At 20 years and 2 months he'd obliterate Vettel's youngest-champion record (23) in only his second season, with five rounds to spare — one shy of Schumacher's 2002 record. 340 points, 8 wins, a 102-point lead. Even if it doesn't land in Austin, Mexico is a near-formality.
  • Verstappen's late-season surge — Singapore finally fell to him last time out (the one track he'd never won), his second win in three races, and now he arrives at a circuit where he's won 4 of the last 5. A fifth COTA win ties Hamilton's track record. He's jumped from 6th to 4th in the standings in two rounds — the form driver of the autumn.
  • The P2 scrap is genuinely alive — Russell 238, Hamilton 229, Verstappen 221, Leclerc 215, Norris 201: five drivers covered by 37 points for best-of-the-rest honors. Russell's engine penalty disaster in Singapore (started from the back) let the pack close right up.
  • Red Bull hunting McLaren for P3 — Verstappen's wins plus Hadjar's steady scoring have Red Bull closing hard on a stagnating McLaren in the constructors — Piastri hasn't out-scored Norris in months and papaya has gone quiet since summer. Mercedes (578) long since checked out front; Ferrari holds P2.
  • Cadillac's home debut — First US GP for the all-American team. Still zero points from Pérez and Bottas in year one, but 400k Texans will make it feel like a win regardless. Expect maximum hoopla, minimum lap time.
  • Triple-header opener — Austin starts three races in 15 days: Mexico City next weekend, then Interlagos Nov 6–8. If Antonelli doesn't clinch here, the title could be decided at your home race.
