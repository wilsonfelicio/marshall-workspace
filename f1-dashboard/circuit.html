Where it gets decided: Almost everything happens into T1 — a 1.07 km tow down the main straight into the only genuinely heavy braking zone, with the T16 exit before it deciding who gets the run. The T6 tight left and the T10 left-hander are the secondary lunges. The lap is really won in the T12–13–14 triple-apex right: carry speed there and you gain three tenths and a better run home — but it hammers the front-left, which is why Pirelli mandates two stops. With strategy forced into the same windows for everyone, qualifying and tyre offset late in stints are everything; sand off the racing line and regular safety cars add the chaos variable.
