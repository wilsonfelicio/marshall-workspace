Qualifying is ~70% of the job here: with walls on every apex and only two honest braking-zone passes (T7 and T13), track position rules. The 2023 removal of the old grandstand chicane created the DRS 4 flat-out stretch and cut ~8s off the lap, but it's still the slowest average-speed race of the year — 62 laps, nearly two hours, 30°C and 80% humidity at night. Until 2024, every single edition had a safety car.
