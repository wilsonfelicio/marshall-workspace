Las Vegas Strip Circuit — Paradise, Nevada

6.201 km · 17 turns · 50 laps · Counterclockwise · Night race · Lap record 1:33.365 (Verstappen, 2025) · Simplified layout

Where it gets decided: Everything funnels into T14 — a ~1.9 km tow down the Strip ending in the biggest braking zone of the year, from ~350 km/h to second gear. Defend there and you're exposed into T1 next; the undercut is weak because warm-up on cold asphalt takes two laps, so most passes happen on track, late in stints, on tyre delta. The Sphere section (T5–T9) is where lap time is found — slow, technical, and the only part of the lap that punishes a skinny-wing setup. Graining the front-left on the long left-handers is the race-killer; managing it decides who's alive at the end when the safety car (three for three editions) shuffles the deck.
