Mexico is a drag-race circuit wearing a technical disguise. At 2,285 m the air is ~25% thinner, so cars bolt on maximum-downforce wings and still slide around on Monza-level grip — while hitting the highest top speeds of the year on the run to T1. Braking distances stretch, brakes and power units cook, and the tow is enormous, which makes T1 the single best passing opportunity on the calendar. The esses reward rhythm, the Foro Sol stadium section is slow, dusty and decisive for the final sector, and T17 — the surviving half of the legendary banked Peraltada — must be taken flat to carry speed onto the 1.2 km straight. Track position helps, but nobody is safe into Turn 1.
