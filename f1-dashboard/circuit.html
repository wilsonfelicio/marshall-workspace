The Hungaroring packs 14 corners into 4.38 km with barely a straight to breathe. Turn 1 — a downhill right at the end of the main straight — is the one place a pass really sticks; the twisting middle sector (the flowing esses through Turns 6–8 and the slow left of Turns 10–11) rewards a planted, high-downforce car and punishes the smallest error. It's often called "Monaco without walls": qualifying position is worth its weight in gold.
