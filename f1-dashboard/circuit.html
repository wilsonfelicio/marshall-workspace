A lap of Baku: launch past the pits and hold it flat for ~2.2 km along the Caspian seafront — the longest full-throttle stretch in F1, nudging 360 km/h in the slipstream — before the monster stop into T1, the lap's prime passing zone. T2 and the second boost zone feed the T3 stop, move number two. A run of 90° city blocks leads to the signature: the castle section (T8–T12), 7.6 m wide, uphill, threading the 12th-century Icherisheher walls in second gear — the tightest squeeze on the calendar, where one clipped apex ends the weekend. From there the track falls back toward the sea: the bumpy T13 kink, the downhill T15 stop that invites lunges, then the flat-out T16–T20 sweeps where exit speed decides whether you attack or defend for the next 2.2 km. Monaco walls, Monza speeds — that's why nothing here ever ends quietly.
