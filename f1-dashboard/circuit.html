Where it gets decided: You can't win the race at Senna S but you can lose it — the downhill braking zone off the pit straight with DRS 1 is the primary overtake point, and lap-1 contact is a near-tradition. Second move is at T4 Descida do Lago, usually a slipstream pass off Reta Oposta. The real strategic corner is T12 Juncão: get a good exit and the 1.2 km uphill drag through Subida dos Boxes puts you right on the car ahead for Senna S next lap. Short lap (around 70s) means strategy swings fast — undercuts bite and the pit window is narrow.
