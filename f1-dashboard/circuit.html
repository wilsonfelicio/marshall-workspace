Zandvoort threads 14 corners through the North Sea dunes with barely a flat moment — a genuine roller-coaster. It's the only F1 track with banked corners: the Hugenholtzbocht (Turn 3) and the flat-out final Arie Luyendyk bend (Turn 14) are banked at ~18°, and that last one is the key — carrying speed through it fires cars down the DRS main straight and sets up the one real passing move, into the banked Tarzan hairpin (Turn 1). Everywhere else the narrow ribbon makes overtaking brutal, so qualifying position is worth its weight in gold — and this Sprint weekend, there are two grids to nail.
