COTA is a momentum circuit disguised as a stop-start one: the esses reward a stable front end, but races are decided at the two big braking zones — T1 after the hill and T12 after the back straight. The T11 hairpin exit sets up the whole DRS run, so defending there chains into the next corner. Built on expansive Texas clay, the surface bumps shift every year and punish stiff setups. Expect track-limits penalties at T9 and T19 — there are almost always a few.
