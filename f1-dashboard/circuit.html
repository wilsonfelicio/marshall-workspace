Monza is four full-throttle blasts stitched together by three braking events. The lap: 1.1 km down the main straight at ~360 km/h into the brutal Rettifilo chicane (the season's heaviest braking and the prime passing move), flat through the majestic Curva Grande, another hard stop at the Roggia chicane, then the twin Lesmos — the only "normal" corners on the track. The downhill Serraglio run feeds the Ascari complex, the lap's one rhythm section, before the back straight and the endless Parabolica, where exit speed decides your fate all the way down the main straight. Thin wings, tow games in qualifying, slipstream trains in the race — pure speed, nothing else.
