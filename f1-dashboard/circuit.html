A lap of the brand-new Madring (layout simplified — nobody has raced it yet): full noise down the main straight past the IFEMA halls into the heavy T1 stop, the lap's prime passing zone. A second straight-mode blast into T4 gives move number two. The Subida de las Cárcavas climbs into the fast Valdebebas esses — the commitment section — before the track dives through twin tunnels beneath the M-11 motorway. Then the signature: La Monumental, a 550 m semicircular bowl banked at 24%, where cars run wide-open for about six seconds inside a 45,000-seat amphitheatre. What the banking gives, the Bunker takes away — a tight, walled, second-gear complex where mistakes meet concrete — before the final corners fire cars back onto the straight, where exit speed decides whether you attack or defend into T1. Designed by Studio Dromo with overtaking as the explicit brief; Sunday is the proof.
