# Mexico Macro — Six-Month Digest

A thematic, chronological synthesis of sell-side research on the Mexican economy
(**18 Dec 2025 – 26 Jun 2026**), built from **118 unique J.P. Morgan and Citi notes**.

> **Strictly private & confidential.** Synthesised from copyrighted source research
> (J.P. Morgan, Citi). Source PDFs and extracted/trimmed text are intentionally **excluded**
> from this repository (see `.gitignore`). Not investment advice.

## Contents
- **`Mexico_Macro_Digest.html`** — the report. Open in any browser; *Print → Save as PDF* for a shareable PDF. Eight themes, each read in chronological order, with a custom rate-path chart and inline citations.
- **`00_Chronological_Index.md`** — index of all 118 source notes (date · source · themes · title).
- **`01_Banxico_Monetary_Policy.md`** — Banxico chapter (markdown source).

## Themes
Banxico / monetary · Inflation · Growth & activity · Trade / USMCA / tariffs ·
Balance of payments, FX & external · Fiscal & sovereign ratings ·
Politics, security & institutions · Pemex & energy.

## Method
Every claim derives solely from the source documents, cited inline as `[House DocID, date]`,
with page numbers on direct quotes. Where J.P. Morgan and Citi diverged or revised their views,
the evolution is tracked rather than smoothed over.
